About

Hi! I'm Supriyo Chatterjee, a professional consultant physiotherapist. I lead a team of experienced and renowned physiotherapists dedicated to providing high-quality treatment.

Professional Background

Expertise that drives better recovery.

Led by an experienced physiotherapist and trainer, we provide specialized support in sports physiotherapy and spinal care. Our expertise includes advanced techniques like dry needling and myofascial release, ensuring effective pain management, improved mobility, and long-term recovery for every patient.

Why Choose Us

Delivering trusted care with expertise, compassion, and a focus on your complete recovery.

At Physio Supriyo, we believe in delivering Quality First (Non Compromised Quality Service) through state-of-the-art infrastructure and personalized treatment plans. Our focus on posture correction, drug-free therapy, and advanced technology ensures effective recovery, while our structured prehab & rehab programmes and expert Maitland mobilization techniques help you move better, feel stronger, and live pain-free.

Special Care For

We provide dedicated care for neurological and musculoskeletal conditions including CVA (stroke) patients, Parkinson’s disorder, arthritis, and frozen shoulder. Our goal is to restore movement, improve strength, and help patients regain independence in their daily lives.
